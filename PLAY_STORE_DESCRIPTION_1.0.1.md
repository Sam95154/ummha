# MOMIN — Where Faith Meets Devotion

MOMIN is an Islamic daily companion that brings everyday worship tools together in one place.

## Features

- **Prayer times and reminders:** View daily prayer times and use available Azan and prayer reminders.
- **Quran:** Read the Holy Quran with available translations and reading tools.
- **Duas:** Browse supplications for everyday moments.
- **Qibla:** Find the direction of the Qibla.
- **Tasbeeh:** Keep track of dhikr and remembrance.
- **Islamic calendar:** View Islamic dates and important occasions.
- **Mosque information:** Explore mosque information and nearby options where location services are available.
- **Personal profile:** Manage your account and profile details.
- **Notifications:** Receive app announcements and reminders according to your device permissions and settings.
- **Support:** Submit a complaint or support request and follow its status.

MOMIN is designed to keep useful Islamic tools accessible through a calm, readable experience on mobile devices.

Location permission is used for location-dependent features such as prayer-time settings, Qibla and nearby mosque information. Feature availability may vary by location, device permissions and app version.
