The person behind MOMIN

About the Founder

A personal introduction to the creator of MOMIN — an Islamic companion built around accessible worship tools, thoughtful design, and technology in service of faith.

Founder & Creator

Shaik Sameer Basha

✦ Artificial Intelligence and Data Science Engineer

I’m Shaik Sameer Basha, the creator of MOMIN. I built MOMIN with the aim of bringing everyday Islamic tools together in one accessible place, combining my interest in technology with a commitment to making faith-supporting features easier to use.

My vision for MOMIN is to provide a calm, approachable experience where Muslims can reach useful worship tools, Quran reading, supplications and Islamic information without unnecessary complexity.

Where Faith Meets Devotion

The idea

MOMIN brings practical Islamic tools together in one place, helping users access prayer timings, Quran reading, duas, Tasbeeh, Qibla tools, and Islamic calendar information.

The vision

To make daily worship tools clear and convenient to use, with a peaceful design that respects the importance of faith and personal privacy.
