Delete your MOMIN account

You can request deletion here even if you have uninstalled MOMIN. You do not need to reinstall the app. We first verify that you control the email address connected to the account.

  1. Enter the email address used for your MOMIN account. We will send a one-time sign-in link.
  2. Open the link in the same browser where you made the request.
  3. Review the account shown and confirm deletion. The request is then sent to the protected deletion service.

This action is permanent. Your MOMIN authentication account, profile, profile image files, complaints and attachments, complaint replies, notification records, device records, push tokens and subscriptions will be deleted. A limited contribution-ledger record may be retained without its account identifier for accounting, audit, fraud-prevention or legal purposes. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Having trouble receiving the verification link? Email MOMIN Support to request account deletion. Never send your password or a one-time sign-in link to support.
