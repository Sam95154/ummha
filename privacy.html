Privacy Policy

This policy explains how MOMIN (also known as Noor-ul-Islam in parts of the service) handles information when you use the MOMIN website and app.

Effective date: 3 November 2026 · Last updated: 5 November 2026
Request account deletionContact privacy support
Feature availability: Giving campaigns, fundraising, donation instructions and contribution tracking are disabled and unavailable in this release. Existing historical financial records may remain in restricted records for accounting, audit, dispute-resolution or legal purposes as described below.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

The MOMIN project (the “MOMIN service”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is responsible for the information described in this policy. MOMIN is an Islamic daily companion with prayer-time tools, Quran content, duas, Qibla and mosque features, notifications, user profiles, support complaints and account information.

For privacy questions, access or correction requests, or help with account deletion, contact mominsupport0@gmail.com. Do not email passwords, one-time codes or authentication links.

2. Information we handle

Account and profile information

When you create or use a MOMIN account, we process the account identifier and information supplied by your sign-in provider, which may include your name, email address, profile photo URL, sign-in provider and email-verification status. Optional profile details you choose to add may include phone number, birthday, gender and profile photo. Google sign-in may provide your name, email address and profile photo according to the information available from Google and the permissions you grant.

Support and information you submit

If you submit a complaint or support request, we process its category, subject, description, status, timestamps, support replies and any attachment you choose to upload. The current complaint form accepts images and PDF files. Authorised support personnel may access the information needed to respond.

Device, session and notification information

To protect accounts and deliver notifications, MOMIN may process a random device key, device name, browser or operating-system user-agent, session identifier, push-notification token or browser push endpoint, notification preferences, delivery status and technical error information. Notification records may contain a title, message, image, route and delivery/read timestamps.

Location and prayer settings

If you grant location permission or enter a city, MOMIN may use device coordinates or your selected city and country to calculate prayer times, determine Qibla direction, find nearby mosques and prepare prayer reminders. When background prayer notifications are enabled, the selected location, city, country, timezone, prayer calculation preferences and prayer schedule may be stored with the notification subscription so reminders can be prepared. You can deny or revoke location permission in your device or browser settings; location-dependent features may then be unavailable.

Preferences stored on your device

Theme, language, selected Quran chapter or translation, reading position, prayer-progress selections and other app preferences may be stored locally in browser or app storage. Local information may remain on that device after remote account deletion. Clear the app/browser storage or uninstall the app to remove locally stored information from that device.

Historical financial records

Historical contribution-ledger entries already held by MOMIN may include an amount, currency, fund category, transaction or provider reference, payment or receipt status and relevant timestamps. These records are not exposed as an active user-facing contribution feature in this release. Giving campaigns, donation instructions and new campaign/contribution operations are disabled. MOMIN does not collect card or bank credentials through an in-app donation checkout.

3. Third-party services and APIs

MOMIN uses third-party services for hosting, authentication, notifications, prayer calculations, Quran data, geocoding and selected convenience features. When your use of a feature causes a request to one of these providers, the provider may receive technical information such as your IP address, request time, device/browser information and the query or coordinates required for that feature. The provider’s own privacy policy and terms also apply to its processing.

Supabase — authentication, database, profile and support records, file storage and server functions. Supabase Privacy Policy.
Google and Firebase — Google account sign-in, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) when push notifications are enabled, Google Input Tools when supported transliteration is requested, and Google Maps when you choose to open a map search. Google Privacy Policy.
Render — hosting and serving the MOMIN website. Render Privacy Policy.
AlAdhan — prayer-time and Qibla API requests, which may include coordinates or city/country information needed to return results. AlAdhan privacy information.
BigDataCloud, OpenStreetMap Nominatim and Photon by Komoot — reverse-geocoding requests that convert device coordinates into a readable locality for location features. BigDataCloud, OpenStreetMap Foundation and Komoot.
Quran.com API and AlQuran.cloud — Quran translation catalogues, translation text, Arabic Quran text and transliteration requested by the Quran reader. Quran.com and AlQuran.cloud.
jsDelivr and Google Fonts — delivery of the authentication page’s Supabase JavaScript library and web fonts. jsDelivr privacy information and Google Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with service providers as needed to operate requested features, respond to support requests, protect the service or comply with applicable law. We do not control third-party providers or guarantee their independent availability or practices.

4. Why we use information

  • To create, authenticate and maintain your account and profile.
  • To provide prayer times, Qibla, nearby mosque results, Quran content, duas and other features you request.
  • To prepare and deliver prayer reminders and notifications according to your permissions and preferences.
  • To receive and respond to complaints, support requests and attachments.
  • To retain limited historical financial records where required for accounting, audit, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution or legal purposes. The current release does not expose fundraising or contribution features.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, diagnose errors and keep the service reliable.
  • To meet applicable legal, regulatory, accounting or dispute-resolution obligations.

5. Permissions and your choices

Location, notification and exact-alarm permissions are requested for features that need them. You can change these permissions in Android or browser settings, and you can change notification preferences within MOMIN. Revoking a permission may prevent the related feature from working but does not automatically delete your MOMIN account.

6. Retention and account deletion

We keep account and profile information while your account is active and use it for the purposes described in this policy. You may request deletion through the in-app account settings or through the public MOMIN account-deletion page. The external page lets you start a request without reinstalling the app. It verifies control of the account email before showing the final deletion confirmation.

After you complete the verified deletion confirmation, the account authentication record, MOMIN profile, profile image files, device records, push tokens and subscriptions, notification and delivery records, complaints, complaint replies and complaint attachments associated with the account are deleted by the account-deletion service.

Limited financial-record exception (historical records only): a contribution-ledger entry already held by MOMIN may be retained for accounting, audit, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution or legal purposes. This release does not offer active fundraising or contribution tracking. When an account is deleted, its account identifier is detached from a retained record where the applicable deletion process requires this. Limited remaining fields may include amount, currency, fund category, transaction/provider reference, payment or receipt status and timestamps. Retention does not make the record an active user profile or an active campaign.

Information held in routine service-provider backups or security logs may remain for a limited period under the provider’s retention schedules and applicable law. Such copies are access-restricted and are not used to keep your deleted account active. Locally stored preferences or reading progress on your device are not removed by remote account deletion.

If a deletion request cannot be completed, the page will show that it failed. Contact MOMIN Support for assistance. Never send a password or one-time sign-in link.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect information, including HTTPS connections, authenticated account access, database access controls and server-side functions for privileged account operations. No website, app, network or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your device and email account secure and do not share authentication links or verification codes.

8. Your privacy requests

You may ask us to access, correct or delete personal information associated with your account, or raise a privacy concern, by emailing mominsupport0@gmail.com. You can also use the account-deletion page. We may need to verify that you control the relevant account before disclosing or changing account information. We handle requests in accordance with applicable privacy laws, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and rules to the extent applicable and in force.

9. Children

MOMIN is a general Islamic companion. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that is not permitted by applicable law or local requirements, contact us so we can review the concern.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, its service providers or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published. Please review this page periodically.
